WHO WE ARE

Two friends. One mission.

A&T Solutions was founded by two lifelong friends who turned years of real-world operational experience into training that helps civilian communities prevent attacks and improve public safety.

"We took the operational knowledge and real-world experience we'd built over the years, and turned it into something communities anywhere could actually use."

TOMER OREN — Co-Founder, A&T Solutions
ORIGIN

Built by two people who've done the work.

Tomer and Amit have known each other since childhood. Both built years of hands-on experience in operational and security roles within high-pressure, real-world environments — Tomer went on to work as an instructor, training military and security forces internationally.

As Amit's path in that world was drawing to a close, the two of them saw an opportunity: to take the operational knowledge and real-world experience they had built over the years and pass it on — not to other professionals, but to civilian communities.

Together, they founded A&T Tactical Solutions.

OUR MISSION

Prevent incidents. Prepare communities. Save lives.

Our mission is to take real operational knowledge and experience and pass it on to civilian communities around the world — helping prevent attacks and improve public safety.

PREVENT

Stop incidents before they start

We focus first on prevention — effective planning, situational awareness, and making optimal use of the surrounding environment.

PREPARE

Build local response capability

Beyond training individuals, we help establish local rapid-response and emergency preparedness teams where they're needed.

PROTECT

Reduce chaos, save lives

When incidents do happen, our training is built to increase confidence, reduce chaos, and improve outcomes for everyone involved.

WHEN AN INCIDENT OCCURS

Training built for the moments that matter most.

Should an incident occur, our training is designed to carry people through it — calmly, effectively, and with the best possible outcome.

  • 01

    Confident, effective response

    Responders are trained to manage high-stress situations with minimal chaos and minimal casualties.

  • 02

    Immediate lifesaving care

    If injuries occur, responders can provide immediate lifesaving medical care while the incident is still unfolding.

  • 03

    Proper evacuation & coordination

    We teach correct casualty evacuation procedures and coordination with emergency services and authorities.
